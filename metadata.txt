description=Three map tools: measure distance and bearing between two points, draw an equidistant radius ring, and compute a terrain viewshed from a DEM.
about=The plugin provides three map tools, all activated from the toolbar and finished with right-click or Esc.<br><br>(1) Distance and bearing: click point A then point B; a label at point B shows the ellipsoidal distance, the initial bearing in degrees from true north (0-360) and its Chinese compass name. Moving the mouse after picking A previews the measurement live.<br><br>(2) Radius ring: click a centre point, move the mouse to grow an equidistant circle; the radius is labelled on the circle. Every direction has exactly the requested ground distance (Vincenty direct solution).<br><br>(3) Viewshed: choose a DEM once via "Set elevation (DEM) layer", then click an observation point and drag to set the view distance. The plugin reads the ground elevation at that point from the DEM and computes which cells are actually visible from it, drawing the result as a green overlay. Ray casting with a maximum-elevation test is used, with earth curvature correction.<br><br>All annotations are anchored to map coordinates and follow panning and zooming. No external dependencies and no network access.
