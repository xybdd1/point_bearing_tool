about=Click point A and then point B on the map canvas. The result label is drawn at point B and shows the ellipsoidal distance from A to B, the initial bearing in degrees from true north (0-360) and its Chinese compass name. Moving the mouse after picking A previews the measurement live. The label is anchored to the map coordinate and follows panning and zooming. Right-click or press Esc to finish; the annotation stays on the map until you choose "Clear measurement" from the Plugins menu.<br><br>Distances are computed on the WGS84 ellipsoid with QgsDistanceArea; bearings are great-circle initial bearings. No external dependencies and no network access.
