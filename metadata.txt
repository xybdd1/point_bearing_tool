description=Click two points on the map to measure the ellipsoidal distance and the bearing from A to B, with the result labelled directly on the map at the end point.
about=Two map tools are provided. (1) Distance and bearing: click point A and then point B; the result label is drawn at point B and shows the ellipsoidal distance, the initial bearing in degrees from true north (0-360) and its Chinese compass name. Moving the mouse after picking A previews the measurement live. (2) Radius ring: click a centre point, then move the mouse to grow an equidistant circle around it; the radius is labelled on the circle. Click again to fix the circle. Both annotations are anchored to map coordinates and follow panning and zooming. Right-click or press Esc to finish; annotations stay on the map until you choose "Clear all annotations".<br><br>Distances are computed on the WGS84 ellipsoid with QgsDistanceArea; circles are generated with Vincenty direct solutions so the ground distance is exactly the requested radius in every direction. No external dependencies and no network access.
